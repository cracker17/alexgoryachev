Agentic AI — software that takes actions rather than just answering questions — will change university operations before it changes the classroom. Enrollment, advising, financial aid, and research administration are the first workflows where it runs end to end. Alex tells university leaders to pilot in operations first, where the savings are countable, and to write governance rules before the first agent goes live.

The future of education is personalized, lifelong, and AI-assisted, and human mentorship becomes the scarcest thing a campus sells. Within a decade most students will study alongside an AI tutor and most campuses will run administrative work through AI agents. The institutions that treat AI as infrastructure will pull away from the ones treating it as a threat.

The clearest example is the California State University system's $17M ChatGPT Edu deployment, reaching 22 campuses and 460,000 students.
